# LLB Realty Chennai — Plots & Independent Homes Portal

An ultra-realistic, luxury web portal designed with a **90% White & 10% Black** architectural aesthetic for **LLB Realty & Promoters**, showcasing residential layout plots and compact family homes across Chennai, India. Fully responsive on all mobile smartphones and desktop screens.

---

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Features

### 1. Front Landing Page & Google Maps Style Navigation Transition
- **Realistic House Front Facade**: High-definition architectural photography of a modern residential home.
- **Luxury LLB Logo Crest**: A central gold-and-black geometric emblem featuring the iconic **LLB** insignia with an interactive pulse ring.
- **Google Maps Navigation Move (Right-to-Left Sweep)**:
  - When the LLB logo is clicked, an animated **Google Maps Navigation HUD & Radar Sweep** triggers.
  - The map camera pans across Chennai from **Right to Left** (East Coastal OMR ➔ Central KK Nagar / MGR Nagar ➔ West Maduravoyal / Koyambedu).
  - A glowing GPS navigation puck with radar pulse glides along an SVG route polyline with real-time coordinate readouts.
  - Landmark tags light up as the marker sweeps across, concluding with a target lock and audio chime before smoothly unveiling the **Chennai Corridors Map & Properties Discovery** page.

### 3. Live Google Maps Driving Directions & Navigation
- **Route Button on Every Property Card**: 1-click button to open turn-by-turn driving directions from your GPS location directly to the house or plot.
- **Full Navigation Suite in Details Modal**: Includes live GPS detection (`https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=...&destination=...&travelmode=driving`) and direct pin location view.
- **Route in Automated Prospectus**: Included in the email prospectus and Gmail sharing link.

### 4. Elaborated Property Details & Multi-Photo Gallery
- **Multi-Photo Interactive Gallery**: Every property features multiple authentic photos (*Front Elevation, Living Hall, Modular Kitchen, Master Bedroom, and Private Terrace/Plot Layout*) with thumbnail switching.
- **Extensive Specifications**:
  - Land/Plot Area vs. Built-up Area
  - Vasthu Facing (East / North)
  - Groundwater Depth (Sweet potable water at 16–25 ft)
  - CMDA & RERA Approval numbers with 100% Clear Title verification
  - Construction Materials & Fixtures (Teak main door, 4x2 Vitrified tiles, Jaguar CP fittings)
  - Key Proximity Matrix (distance to Metro, railway station, schools, hospitals)
  - Pre-approved Bank Loan details (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis up to 90%)
